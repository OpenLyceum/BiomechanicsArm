PASCO USB probe: Wireless Force Acceleration (PS-3202) and Rotary Motion (PS-3220)

Bring-up tool, not part of the sim. It finds out how a sensor presents itself over USB and whether the sim's USB transport (written for the PS-3219 motion sensor) works on it. Use Chrome or Edge, served from npm start (http://localhost:5173/scripts/probe/usb-probe.html).

  1. Close Capstone and SPARKvue. Plug one sensor in with its micro-USB cable and switch it on.
  2. Press 1. WebUSB. If the picker is empty, press 2. WebHID instead.
  3. For a force sensor press 3 channels; for a rotary sensor press 1 channel, then turn the shaft.
  4. Press Copy log and send the log back. Repeat with the other sensor.
